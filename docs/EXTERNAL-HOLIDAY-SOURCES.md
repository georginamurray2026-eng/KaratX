## 1. WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT

**THIS DOCUMENT RECORDS WHAT THE RELEASES STATE. NOTHING MORE.**

Filtering by obligation 64's window, the Mon–Thu rule, the treatment of Fridays,
and classification against the Twelve Data feed are all **DERIVATION**, and they
belong in [`OPEN-QUESTIONS-OBLIGATION-64.md`](./OPEN-QUESTIONS-OBLIGATION-64.md).

**TRANSCRIPTION HERE, DERIVATION THERE.** The test that keeps the separation
honest: **a reader must be able to re-derive the pinned set from this document
plus the rules in `OPEN-QUESTIONS-OBLIGATION-64.md`, and this document must not
have pre-applied any of them.** So the tables below carry **the complete
contents, 2020–2027**, unclipped — including years, weekdays and holidays that
no rule in this project will ever select.

That is the point. A corpus filtered to what was wanted is not a corpus; it is
the conclusion with its evidence deleted.

## 7. NYSE IS A DIFFERENT VENUE — DATE NOMINATION ONLY

**NYSE is equities. This instrument is XAU/USD spot metals.** This corpus is used
to **NOMINATE DATES** and is **never evidence of XAU/USD trading hours.**

The measured argument for that restriction is not restated here — it lives in
[`OPEN-QUESTIONS-OBLIGATION-64.md`](./OPEN-QUESTIONS-OBLIGATION-64.md) §3.1,
which shows that NYSE's 1:00 p.m. early close matches the observed close time on
exactly two of the 41 pinned dates, and that all 23 of the 2022-onward truncated
dates close at 14:15 or 14:30 instead.

**So the times in §3, §4 and §5 are transcribed because they are in the releases,
not because they predict anything about this instrument.** Reading a 1:00 p.m.
out of this document and into a `market_holidays` row would be the substitution
§3.1 exists to forbid.

## 8. THERE IS NO METALS CORPUS — CME COMEX COULD NOT BE OBTAINED

**Authoritative historical CME COMEX metals holiday-and-hours material could not
be obtained for 2020–2025.** One CME clearing advisory confirms **2025-01-01** as
a holiday and gives **no trading times and no metals specificity** — one date out
of the 41, with no hours attached.

**Nothing was substituted for it and nothing was extrapolated from it.**

**This document is the NYSE corpus. A metals corpus does not exist**, and the
distance between the two is the standing weakness in the whole proposal side of
obligation 64's rule — not a formality. CME is the venue whose calendar would
actually predict the close times measured in `OPEN-QUESTIONS-OBLIGATION-64.md`
§4, and it is the one source that could turn those observations into an
explanation.

## 9. A MACHINE-READABLE FIXTURE IS LATER WORK, AND IS NOT AN OBLIGATION

A structured fixture derived from this corpus — something a test or a seed script
could read — is **deliberately not opened as an obligation.**

**The corpus had to be read and committed before anything could be designed on
top of it.** Opening a row for a design whose input did not yet exist is how a
shape gets fixed before its evidence is seen. The input now exists; the design
decision does not, and it should be made against §3–§6 rather than against a
memory of them.
